On this page you can view detailed information about the albums in my collection. This includes the album and artist name, in the same alphabetical order as the collection page, the media type of the album, where it can be sourced from, where I purchased it from, how much I paid for it, and how common or rare it is to find. For the cost column, if their is parenthesis, then the number inside the parenthesis is the approximate cost of the album if it were to be purchased from a resale site, such as eBay or Discogs, and the number outside of the parenthesis is how much I paid for it. The squiggly line (or tilda ~) means that the number is an approximation, and the number is not exact. The availability column is based on how easy it is to find the album, and is based on searches I conducted while building this page, using sites like Amazon, eBay, Discogs, and the artist's own website. If the album is listed as common, then it is easy to find and purchase. If it is listed as uncommon, then it is a little more difficult to find and purchase. If it is listed as rare, then it is very difficult to find and purchase.

You'll see that a lot of these albums I have purchased from the artist directly, typically when the album came out. I enjoy this method as not only does it support the artist directly, but often times you can buy box sets with merch, or get an original pressing of the album, with variations to its track list, mixing, or even exclusive physical songs. Many of the Amazon purchases were gifts I received, but I would highly encourage you to go to record stores and purchase there, as it supports local businesses, and ensure that your album won't be damaged or shipping. Just be careful not to get a bootleg, as some record stores sell them along side official pressing. Discogs or rateyourmusic.com are great sites to check the official pressings of albums, and to compare pricing with resale sites.

For the rare entries in my collection, these are either albums that were only sold for a limited time by the artist (Westside Gunn is notorius for doing this), or albums that my parents gave me, such as the Orignal Master Recordings of Pink Floyd's The Wall, which I didn't even know was so rare or special. If you want to purchase a rare album, definitely get ready to spend some money, as the Pray For Paris CD I bought off of eBay was $60, which is as much as some vinyl records, and there is a vinyl for that album, but it is crazy pricey. Thankfully, most albums are an affordable price, and aren't too hard to source, so you can build your own collection without breaking the bank.
