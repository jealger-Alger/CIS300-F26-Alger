What is this site all about?

Welcome to Jacob's Music Collection! This site serves two purposes: showcasing my personal music collection, and encouraging you to start your own collection.

For several years, I have been collecting both vinyl records and CDs, and I love having some of my favorite albums in physical form. I think owning music is not only a great way to support the artists you love, but also ensures that you could listen to your music collection at any time. With the age of streaming, it's easy to forget that you don't own the music on your phone anymore. But if you make your own collection, you can truly say you own your favorite music, and it makes for great decoration!

On this website, you can view my collection, where and how I source my albums, the highlights of my collection, and information on how to contact me and share you own collection and/or ask questions about mine.

I am playing the song "Nights" by Frank Ocean off of his hit 2016 album "Blond" on my record player. I like this record player because it can also play CDs and even cassette tapes (if I owned any) all hooked up to the same speakers, tucked behind the set.

Album of the Week!

This album, or rather albums, were released back-to-back in February of 2025 by one of my favorite hip-hop duos, Paris Texas. They released this rock / rap double EP while on the Chromakopia World Tour, headlining for Tyler, The Creator.

This pair of albums includes several short and hard-hitting bangers, and the record's gatefold art is some of my favorite in my collection. Which is especially cool considering that both albums fit on a single disc meaning a typical gatefold record is uncommon. But then again, Paris Texas themselves are uncommon. Definitely check these albums out when you have time, as Paris Texas deserve to have more fans.
